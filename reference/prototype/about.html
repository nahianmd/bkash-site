The Revolution

bKash started with one core idea: everyone should have the power to control their own money.

With its unmatched last-mile reach, that one idea changed how Bangladesh transacts. Today, millions use bKash to access crucial financial services, turning everyday inclusion into an entire financial ecosystem.
